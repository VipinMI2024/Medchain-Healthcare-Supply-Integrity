# MedChain: Healthcare Integrity Supply Platform

![MedChain Dashboard](https://github.com/VipinMI2024/Medchain-Healthcare-Supply-Integrity/blob/main/Screenshot%202025-07-12%20145836.png)

## 🚀 Project Overview

**MedChain** is a smart, cloud-based platform designed to detect counterfeit medicines, streamline pharmaceutical shipment validations, and ensure compliance with global GS1 standards — all without using expensive sensors or blockchain technologies.

Developed during the Infosys Global Hackathon 2025 by **Team Three Bug Busters**, the platform enhances trust, compliance, and speed in pharma supply chains.

---

## 🧠 Problem Statement

Modern pharmaceutical supply chains face major risks:

- **Fake medicines** can infiltrate shipments.
- **Shipment errors or delays** are often detected too late.
- **Inconsistent data formats** across stakeholders.
- **Global standards (GS1)** are not properly validated.

Currently, no lightweight system exists to check this data and flag risks — especially without costly hardware or tech stacks.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**MedChain** provides an easy-to-use dashboard to:

- Upload shipment data (CSV/Excel)
- Validate GS1 product codes
- Detect missing, invalid, or duplicate entries
- Flag unusual or suspicious shipment patterns
- Export reports and insights
- All hosted on the cloud, with **no sensors or blockchain**

---

- 📦 No sensors, blockchain, or IoT devices required
